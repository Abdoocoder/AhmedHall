# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Municipality administrative staff — booking managers, department heads, and facility coordinators who process hall reservation requests. Internal users managing municipal event spaces. The public-facing booking request form serves external users (citizens, organizations) but the primary interface is the employee dashboard.

## Product Purpose

A comprehensive system for managing event hall bookings in municipality facilities. Enables staff to create, edit, track, and report on hall reservations, manage room and organization records, process public booking requests, and monitor payment status — all in a full Arabic RTL interface with Nabataean calendar support.

## Brand Personality

Official, efficient, unpretentious. This is a municipal tool — it should feel trustworthy and capable, not flashy or trendy. Calm confidence. No gamification, no celebration of routine operations. Every interaction reduces friction for the staff member processing the next booking.

## Anti-references

- Overly dark "developer tool" aesthetics (Terminal black backgrounds, neon accents)
- Corporate SaaS landing page design (3-column feature grids, floating nav, heavy gradients)
- AI-generated generic dashboards (purple/blue gradients, glassmorphism, "smart" badges)
- Consumer app playfulness (illustrations, avatars, gamification elements)

## Design Principles

1. **Arabic-first, not Arabic-translated.** Layout, spacing, and reading flow are designed for RTL natively. English support is secondary.
2. **Reduce cognitive load.** Municipal staff process bookings under time pressure. Every screen should make the next action obvious without reading.
3. **Consistency over novelty.** Same patterns for same operations throughout. No one-off interactions.
4. **Trust through restraint.** No decorative elements that don't serve the task. White space and clean hierarchy communicate reliability.
5. **Progressive disclosure.** Complex operations (booking conflicts, payment tracking) reveal detail gradually.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum contrast
- Full keyboard navigation for all CRUD operations
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout
- RTL support with proper text alignment and logical flow
